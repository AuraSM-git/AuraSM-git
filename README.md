## About Me

I started as an English teacher in 2017 at Kids&Us and Puzzle Consultores, teaching kids & teens on the one hand, and company employees on the other, using CEFR frameworks and Cambridge standards. Then I discovered HTML and CSS, and, diving into JS, PHP, and especially Python, got hooked on backend coding too.

Teaching didn't disappear; it just changed subjects: explaining clearly, breaking problems into steps, staying patient: same muscles, new material. Now I put that into programming, currently doing an internship building scrapers, automation tools, and AI-powered systems.

- Currently still working at Kids&Us and doing an Internship at IslasSEM
- Developing apps on **RAG pipelines** and **web scraping system**
- Learning to combine **AI/LLMs** with practical automation
- Ask me about language teaching, CEFR/Cambridge assessment, or Python/Web development
- How to reach me: **aurasm90@gmail.com**
- Fun fact: I went from grading essays to grading my own commits
